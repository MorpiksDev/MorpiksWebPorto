**EN**
Morpiks is a multi-solution company that helps businesses adapt, evolve, and grow. From software development and rapid prototyping to procurement, business consulting, custom industrial equipment, furniture, and IoT, we deliver solutions tailored to each business context.

Inspired by the chameleon, we adjust our approach to the environment and needs of every client, while staying consistent in our principles.
